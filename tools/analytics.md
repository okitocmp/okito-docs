# Analytics

**Analytics** in the dashboard shows how visitors respond to your banner:

* how often the banner was shown,
* how many visitors chose **Accept All**, **Reject All** or **Customize**,
* consents by template: GDPR, US laws and IAB TCF,
* trends over time.

Use it to see the effect of changes to your banner's text, layout and theme. Advanced analytics is included from the Standard plan.

The header of the dashboard also shows how many of your plan's monthly pageviews each website has used.

{% hint style="info" %}
These numbers describe consent interactions only. They are not a replacement for your web analytics.
{% endhint %}
