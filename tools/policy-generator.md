# Policy generator

The policy generator drafts a **privacy policy**, **cookie policy** or **terms of use** for your website. Open **Policy Generator** in the dashboard.

1. Choose the website, the document type and the language.
2. Enter your company name, contact email and industry, and choose which sections to include (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, marketing, third-party integrations).
3. Generate the draft, review it, and publish it on your own site. Link it from your banner text.

The policy generator is included from the Beginner plan, with a limit of 7 documents per month. On the Professional plan you can also generate policies through the [REST API](../developers/rest-api.md).

{% hint style="warning" %}
A generated policy is a draft, not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified advisor before you publish it.
{% endhint %}
