# Billing

## Choose or change a plan

Each website has its own plan and subscription. Open **Subscription plans** (or the upgrade button at the top of the dashboard), choose the website and the plan, and pay by card. You can pay monthly or yearly.

* **Upgrade**: takes effect right away. Features such as Google Consent Mode and IAB TCF start working on your site immediately.
* **Downgrade**: features your new plan doesn't include switch off for that website.

## Trial

New accounts can start with a trial of a paid plan. Choose the plan when you sign up.

## Invoices

Invoices and payment methods are under **Billing** (Owner and Admin).

## Failed payments

If a website's payment fails and can't be collected, that website's banner is paused until the payment succeeds. Your other websites keep working.

## Shopify

Shopify stores pay through Shopify App Pricing and manage their plan in the Okito app inside Shopify. See [Shopify](../installation/shopify.md).
