# Agency partners

Agencies manage Okito for their clients from an agency account.

## Become a partner

1. Click **Become an Agency Partner** on the Okito site, or go to [app.okito.com/agency/signup](https://app.okito.com/agency/signup).
2. Verify your email and complete the sign-up steps.
3. You land on the **agency dashboard**, where you add and switch between client workspaces.

## Working with client websites

* Create one Okito website per client domain. Each has its own key, banner, scans, consent records and plan.
* When you add a client site, choose its region (TR or EU). It follows the country you pick, and you can change it before you add the site. The client's website, banner and consent records are stored in that region; your agency account and billing stay in your home region. See [Data regions](../getting-started/data-regions.md).
* Each client website is billed on its own plan, so upgrading one client doesn't affect the others.
* Beginner is included in the client's slot. Moving a client website from Beginner to Standard or Professional opens the Stripe payment page. Moving it from Standard to Professional charges the agency's saved payment method right away, after Okito shows the amount and you confirm; see [Billing](billing.md#choose-or-change-a-plan).
* When handing a site over, invite the client to the workspace, and note where Okito is installed (theme, plugin or GTM) so nobody adds it a second time.
* Removing a client deletes the client workspace and the website linked to that client, with its banner, scans and consent records, ends its plan and frees its slot. The agency's own website, or any other website on the client's domain, is not deleted.
* Deleting a website in **Websites** removes only that website, its banner, scans and consent records, and ends its plan; the agency's other websites on the same domain stay.

## Who sees which client website

* People you invite to a client workspace see that client's website only, not your other clients' websites or the agency's own websites.
* Your agency team (people invited to the agency workspace) sees the agency's websites and every client website, with their agency role.
* A website added by someone on your agency team who has no workspace of their own is shared with the agency when the agency is the only team they are on: you and your agency team see it, client members do not. It stays owned by the person who added it. Someone with an account of their own keeps the websites they add in that account, unless they are an Editor or Admin on your agency team and choose the agency under **Add to**. That website is seen by the agency team, not by client members; it stays theirs and is billed to them, and it does not use a client slot. See [Team and roles](team-and-roles.md#which-websites-each-person-sees).
* Client members cannot delete the website or change its plan; the agency does that.
* Renaming a client website's domain does not change who can see it, and giving another website a client's domain does not show it to that client.
* Client workspaces are never offered under **Add to** when adding a website. A website a client member adds belongs to that member's own account, not to the client workspace; client websites are added by the agency from the agency dashboard.
* A client website added before client access followed the website appears to the client's people once it is linked. Okito links it automatically when the client's domain identifies the website; support links any it cannot.
