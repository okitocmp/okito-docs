# Agency partners

Agencies manage Okito for their clients from an agency account.

## Become a partner

1. Click **Become an Agency Partner** on the Okito site, or go to [app.okito.com/agency/signup](https://app.okito.com/agency/signup).
2. Verify your email and complete the sign-up steps.
3. You land on the **agency dashboard**, where you add and switch between client workspaces.

## Working with client websites

* Create one Okito website per client domain. Each has its own key, banner, scans, consent records and plan.
* Each client website is billed on its own plan, so upgrading one client doesn't affect the others.
* When handing a site over, invite the client to the workspace, and note where Okito is installed (theme, plugin or GTM) so nobody adds it a second time.
