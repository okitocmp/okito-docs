# Preference centre and cookie list

**Manage Preferences** opens the preference centre. It shows:

* each [cookie category](../cookies-and-scripts/cookie-categories.md) with its description and an on/off switch (Necessary is always on),
* the [services](../cookies-and-scripts/services.md) in each category, such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel, each with its own switch,
* the cookies in each category, from your [cookie scan](../cookies-and-scripts/cookie-scanner.md): name, duration and description,
* **Accept All**, **Reject All** and **Save** buttons.

In [IAB TCF](../compliance/iab-tcf.md) mode, the preference centre shows the IAB purposes, special features and the vendor list instead, including legitimate interest objections.

## Cookie list

The cookie list is filled from your scans and any cookies you add by hand in Cookie Manager. To keep it accurate:

* run a new scan after you add tags or pixels,
* move unknown cookies to the right category,
* never leave a marketing cookie in Necessary.

Categories without cookies or services can be hidden, but only if you really don't use them.
