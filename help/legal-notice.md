# Legal notice

Okito is a software tool that helps you collect, store and pass on your visitors' consent choices.

Nothing in this documentation, in the Okito dashboard, in generated policies, in default banner texts or in replies from Okito support is legal or regulatory advice. Using Okito does not by itself make your website or app compliant with any law.

Which consent your site needs, how your banner should be worded and how long you keep data depend on your own processing and obligations. If you need legal assistance, consult a qualified lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction.

Google consent mode, IAB TCF and the other signals Okito sends are ways to pass consent choices to other services. They are not designed to meet any particular legal or regulatory requirement.
